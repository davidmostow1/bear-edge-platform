What The Markets Mean

Plain-English guide for what Bear Edge can currently evaluate from actual source data.

Total Bases

Singles count 1, doubles 2, triples 3, home runs 4. Walks do not count.

Hits

Any official hit. Walks, errors, sacrifices, and hit-by-pitch do not count.

Runs

Player crosses home plate. This depends heavily on lineup spot and teammates.

Pitcher Ks

Pitcher strikeouts. Bear Edge uses season/recent rates and flags missing context.

Parlays

Every leg must be sourced and priced. Same-game or same-player legs are treated as correlation risk.
